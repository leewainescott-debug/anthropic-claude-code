Key corrections this chart forces on the non-labour mapping:
- Fuel Infrastructure is the refinery/manufacturing BUSINESS portfolio.
  The IT estate (cloud, network, data centre, devices, tooling) is the TDD
  portfolio's TDD platform. Never conflate them.
- Distribution sits under Fuel Infrastructure (Future Fuels, Sales &
  Distribution), which resolves the old DIST question.
- Sig-item programs land on real project platforms: CTRM under Commercial
  Fuels, AmPOS under Ampol Retail, EGI under EG Integration.
- Security spend = Cyber portfolio, Cyber platform.
- Data products = Enterprise Data, Data platform.
- No Lubricants platform exists. No ERP/SAP platform exists. No Way4/cards
  platform exists. Those are open questions for Lee, never inventions.
