# The deck storyline

Eight slides. Each one answers a fundamental question an internal audience actually has, in order, and the question is on the slide as the kicker so nobody has to decode the structure. The research base sits behind the deck rather than in front of it; the main deck carries four numbers in total, and everything else, the measurement machinery, the gates, the tests, the throughput evidence, the market detail, moves to the appendix for people who want depth.

**Slide 1. Title.** Value realisation. Turning technology investment into business outcomes we can prove.

**Slide 2. WHAT IS VALUE?** Title: Value is a measured improvement in a business outcome that finance can see. The slide explains it the way you would to a colleague: a business outcome moves, claims cost per case falls, customers stop leaving, a risk goes away; we measured where it started, we can show where it landed, and finance agrees the movement is real. ROI gets two lines: ROI is one way of expressing value once you have it, and a strong ROI number against an outcome the business never cared about is still worthless. One example runs across the bottom of the slide in a single line and returns on every later slide, so the whole deck teaches through one concrete case.

**Slide 3. WHAT IS VALUE REALISATION?** Title: Value realisation makes the promised value actually arrive, and proves it did. Plain explanation: every investment promises value to get funded; realisation is the discipline of making that promise come true, changing how people work so the outcome moves, tracking it against the baseline, and having finance verify the result. Value architecture gets its one plain sentence here as the design half: deciding up front which outcomes the investment must move, from where, owned by whom, so there is something real to realise. One line ties them: architecture draws it, realisation delivers and certifies it.

**Slide 4. WHY DOES IT MATTER?** Title: Most promised value never arrives, and boards now fund only what can be proven. Three numbers, one line each: 88 per cent of transformations miss their original ambition, Bain 2024; two thirds of boards now make further AI funding conditional on proof of return, CloudZero 2026; a dedicated value owner captures 24 per cent more of the value, Bain 2024. Closing line: our clients are being asked for proof they cannot produce, and nobody in their system owns the problem, which is exactly the gap a firm that builds the platforms can own.

**Slide 5. HOW DO WE APPROACH IT?** Title: Define the value, deliver the change, prove the result. Three columns, and this is the whole method at internal altitude. Define: name the outcome, the baseline, the owner and the date before the money moves. Deliver: change how people work, because the technology alone moves nothing. Prove: track against the baseline in the platform's own data and have finance sign the result, keeping forecast and delivered value separate. The worked example runs underneath the three columns. The six evidence tests, the six gates, the causal chain and the throughput and quality evidence all sit behind this slide in the appendix.

**Slide 6. WHAT DO WE SELL?** Title: Three offers cover the life of an investment, entered through a ten day review. Shape, before the money: build or reset the value case. Realise, during: run the discipline while delivery is live. Recover, after: find the value sitting unused in what the client already owns. Underneath, the entry: the value case review takes one live investment and returns an independent view of its credible value in ten days for a fixed fee, and it expands into whichever offer the finding demands.

**Slide 7. HOW DO WE TALK TO CLIENTS ABOUT IT?** Title: Start every client conversation with the outcome, the baseline and the owner. Three questions, not a script: what business outcome is this investment meant to move; what is it today and would finance sign that number; who owns it after go live. Whoever cannot answer becomes the person the client needs in the room, which is how the conversation reaches the CFO and the operating executives without ever leaving the technology relationship.

**Slide 8. WHAT DO WE DO NEXT?** Title: Prove it in three accounts this year. Pick three accounts with a live investment decision and a trusted sponsor; run the ten day review in each; publish the results internally as the training material; productise what repeats. The standard we sell is the standard we hold: the practice's own value case, with baselines and owners, reviewed monthly.

## The appendix

For people who want the depth, in order: the six evidence tests for a credible value claim; the six gates from forecast to booked value; the investment to value chain with the worked example and failure modes; the throughput and quality evidence answering how we know what value AI is driving, Workday, NBER, DORA, Klarna, McKinsey conversion levers; the seven value dimensions; the offer detail for Shape, Realise and Recover; the client conversation in full with the executive role map; the measurement architecture joining platform, operating and finance data; the governance cadence; the commercial model; the account triggers; the first year plan; the full stat bank with sources and confidence flags; the glossary.
