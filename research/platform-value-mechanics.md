# RAW: Platform unlock mechanics, honest funding, strategy-led vs commodity (agent output, 2026-08-19)

## KEY MECHANICS FOUND (named, real)
- Salesforce: Optimizer (free self-service org scan); partner free health checks = commodity lead-gen (24hrs-5 days, checklist); Signature/Premier Success Plans = Salesforce-run, client-paid, Customer Success Score (Product Adoption / Expertise / Technical Health).
- ServiceNow: HealthScan/Configuration Review (consultant-delivered); Now Value methodology (vision → strategic drivers → business outcomes → success measurement); Impact tiers priced as % of subscription (client-paid; UpperEdge flags hidden costs). GuideVision line: projects finish "on deadline, within budget... but after the project is over, a disenchantment often sets in that the value isn't delivering."
- SAP: Pathfinder (sunset) → Signavio Process Insights discovery edition (free, usage-based, GA Feb 2024); Signavio process mining = as-designed vs as-executed; Value Lifecycle Manager + Signavio = process-driven value management; Preferred Success = client-paid.
- Microsoft: FastTrack = genuinely free at 150+ seats for life of subscription (cleanest vendor-funded example); M365 Adoption Score self-service telemetry.
- Workday: Adoption Kit = content library, not assessment; no native adoption tool; partner-delivered, client pays.
- OneStream: MindStream Health Check = ONE WEEK diagnostic, split sessions (IT/platform admins vs Finance/Ops users), deliverables = Technical Findings Doc + Improvement Roadmap (quick wins + long-term). Riveron/HollandParker/SC&H optimization services. PwC partnership.
- Snowflake: Well-Architected Framework, 5 pillars (cost only one), scorecard, Blueprints, 2026 lenses + CoCo AI review; also embedded as AWS custom lens. Capability-oriented not cost audit.
- Databricks: Partner WAF (Feb 2026, ISV-facing); customer Well-Architected Review via partners; Aimpoint "Strategy Accelerator" = use-case discovery + data readiness → roadmap through deployment with value tracking; deviq "4 Weeks to Production".
- AWS: Well-Architected Review is the relevant mechanic (NOT MAP; MAP = migration funding, irrelevant here).

## GAP EVIDENCE (business flavoured)
- Nexthink Soft-WASTE (2023, 6M+ environments): 49.96% of installed software completely unused.
- Zylo 2026: 305 SaaS apps avg; ~46% licensed seats unused; business units control 70% of SaaS spend vs IT 26% (why duplication happens: nobody sees the portfolio).
- Gartner: app rationalization must move "Beyond Cost Savings" (doc 6780334) — Gartner itself signals the cost→strategy pivot.
- WalkMe 2025: firms underestimate actual tech usage by 1,600%; $104M wasted 2024 (their sample).
- AFP 2025: >90% of finance practitioners use spreadsheets weekly for planning/reporting; 96% of FP&A use spreadsheets for planning DESPITE owning ERP/EPM. (The excel-next-to-the-platform pattern, evidenced.)
- "Shadow CRM" (TDEOS 2026): excel/notion trackers holding more trusted data than the official CRM; fixed by turning on a module the company already owned (pattern echo, no client names).
- Surety Systems: "the failure mode is quiet: nothing breaks, the system just sits underutilized while people work around it" (non-blame framing).
- ALA licence trap (OpenText 2026): reconcile THREE views — contracted entitlements, deployed/activated, actually used. Gaps cut both ways (audit exposure + reclaimable value). This is the diagnostic mechanic.

## CONVERSATION MECHANICS (no insult, no upsell)
- Sobel: client must own the new frame; take them on the journey, don't push.
- FusionSpan: underutilisation is an organisational default, not a client failing ("people fit the new tool into current process without disturbing anything, which underutilises it").
- Avoid "wrong"; collaborative discovery language.
- Positioning verbatims: "maximises your ServiceNow investment" (GuideVision); "yield the greatest return on your investment" (Riveron); "activate exponential ROI" (HollandParker); "systems of execution rather than isolated systems of record" (Prudent); Surety's quiet-failure line.

## FUNDING REALITY (honest)
- Salesforce: FY27 Catalyst pays PARTNERS for post-sale activation/consumption ($1B target); not a client-facing grant. Client pays beyond free commodity checks.
- ServiceNow: Impact is client-paid (% of subscription); MDF/SIF partner-facing, deal-conditional.
- SAP: free = automated tooling only; human adoption service client-paid.
- Microsoft: FastTrack genuinely free (150+ seats).
- Workday/OneStream: no vendor funding found; client pays.
- Snowflake: Partner Services Fund tier/cert-gated; WAF tool free.
- Databricks: Velocity "Activate" SPIFF (early adoption in existing customers) = most relevant fund, tier-gated.
- AWS: MAP irrelevant; WA Review sometimes free via AWS SA.
- PLAIN READ: default = client pays for a short sharply-scoped assessment because the value case carries it; partner incentives offset the partner's cost, they are not client grants. Only Microsoft FastTrack + SAP tooling genuinely free.

## STRATEGY-LED vs COMMODITY
- Commodity: licence audit / free health check; checklist, config/security/data quality; procurement flavoured; lead-gen.
- Strategy-led: starts from the outcome logic of the org's strategy (Fujitsu ResultsChain), names value leakage not wasted spend (PwC VRO), prioritises by business-outcome relevance not licence dollars, ends in an operating cadence not a report.
- ISG credibility angle: independence (not selling licences).

## GROUNDED 2-3 WEEK ENGAGEMENT SHAPE
Week 1 diagnostic: reconcile contracted vs activated vs used; run vendor-native usage tooling as objective baseline; split sessions (platform admins/IT vs business users: workarounds, where excel sits next to the platform and why).
Week 2 map to business direction: exec workshop on where the business is going 12-24 months; cross-reference capability inventory against THAT direction, not a generic maturity checklist; prioritise by business-outcome relevance.
Week 3 roadmap and handoff: each item tied to a named business outcome; split "turn on now" (no new build) vs "needs a scoped enablement phase" (natural conversion point); propose lightweight measurement cadence so it's an operating discipline not a one-off report.
